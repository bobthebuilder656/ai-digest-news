  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

    RAG is a technique where an AI model looks up relevant documents from an outside source, like a company's internal files or a live web search, before writing its answer, instead of relying purely on what it memorized during training. It's the difference between a student answering an exam question entirely from memory versus being allowed to quickly check a textbook first. It helps models give more current, accurate answers and lets them cite exactly where the information came from.
