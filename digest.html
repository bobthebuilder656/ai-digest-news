  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Few-Shot Learning

    Few-shot means giving an AI model a handful of examples of exactly what you want directly in your prompt, so it can pick up the pattern on the spot without any retraining. It's like showing a new employee two or three correctly filled-out forms before asking them to fill out the next one themselves, rather than making them guess the format cold.
