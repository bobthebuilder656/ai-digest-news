  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Fine-Tuning

    Fine-tuning is taking an already-trained AI model and training it further on a smaller, specific dataset so it gets better at a particular task or domain, like legal writing, customer support, or a company's internal jargon. It's the AI equivalent of hiring a smart, broadly educated generalist and then sending them through a few weeks of specialized on-the-job training, instead of educating a brand new specialist from kindergarten onward. It's far cheaper and faster than training a model from scratch.
