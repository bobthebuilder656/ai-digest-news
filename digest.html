  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Zero-Shot Learning

    Zero-shot means an AI model can perform a task it was never specifically trained or shown examples for, relying purely on general knowledge it already picked up. Asking an LLM to write a limerick about your job for the first time, with no examples given, and having it just do a reasonable job, is a zero-shot request, similar to asking a well-read friend to improvise something in a style they've never formally practiced, but have absorbed enough from general reading to fake convincingly.
