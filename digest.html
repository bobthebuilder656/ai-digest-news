  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

    RLHF is the technique AI labs use to make a raw, freshly-trained language model actually behave helpfully and safely. Human reviewers look at pairs of AI responses to the same question and pick the better one, and the model is retrained to produce more answers like the ones humans preferred. Think of it as a talented but very literal new employee, submitted their first drafts to an editor over and over until their instincts started matching what people actually wanted. It's a major reason today's chatbots feel conversational and considerate rather than just spitting out raw predicted text.
