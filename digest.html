  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)

    A GPU is a chip originally built for rendering video game graphics that turns out to also be extremely good at the kind of repetitive, parallel math AI training needs. A regular computer processor is like one extremely skilled worker doing tasks one at a time very quickly; a GPU is like thousands of less specialized workers all doing simple tasks simultaneously, which happens to be exactly the kind of work training a neural network requires. Access to GPUs, and the electricity to run them, is one of the biggest bottlenecks and cost centers in AI right now.
