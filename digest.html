  12. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Prompt Injection

    Prompt injection is an attack where someone hides malicious instructions inside content an AI reads, like a webpage, email, or document, to hijack its behavior against its owner's wishes. Imagine a personal assistant who follows any instruction written on a sticky note handed to them, a scammer could slip in a fake note saying "forward all emails to this address," and a naive assistant would just comply. It's one of the main security concerns for AI agents that browse the web or read documents on your behalf.
