  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Generative AI

    Generative AI is the umbrella term for any AI that creates new content, text, images, audio, video, or code, rather than just sorting or analyzing things that already exist. A spam filter that sorts email into "junk" or "not junk" is not generative; a tool that writes you a brand new email from scratch is. It's called "generative" because it generates something that didn't exist a moment ago, the way a chef generates a new dish from raw ingredients rather than just plating a dish someone else already cooked.
