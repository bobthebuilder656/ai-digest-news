    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Embedding

    An embedding is a way of converting a word, sentence, or image into a long list of numbers that captures its meaning, arranged so that similar concepts end up numerically close together. Imagine plotting every word in the dictionary on a giant map where "king" and "queen" end up as close neighbors, and "queen" and "bicycle" end up on opposite sides of the map. That numerical map is the mathematical backbone behind AI search, recommendations, and how models compare meaning rather than just exact spelling.
