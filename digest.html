    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Deepfake

    A deepfake is synthetic media, usually video, images, or audio, generated by AI to convincingly show a real person saying or doing something they never actually did. It's a digital-age evolution of old-fashioned photo doctoring, except instead of touching up a still image with editing software, AI can now fabricate entire moving, talking footage that's difficult to distinguish from the real thing at a glance.
