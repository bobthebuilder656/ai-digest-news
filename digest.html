    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Synthetic Data

    Synthetic data is artificially generated information, often produced by another AI model, used to train or test systems when real-world data is scarce, expensive, or too sensitive to use directly. It's similar to a flight simulator: pilots train on realistic but fake scenarios because letting them practice emergency landings on real planes with real passengers would be far too risky and expensive.
