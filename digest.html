    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Attention (Mechanism)

    Attention is the part of a transformer that decides which words in a sentence matter most to understanding any given word. In the sentence "The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big," attention is how a model figures out that "it" refers to the trophy, not the suitcase, by weighing which earlier words are most relevant. It works a bit like a spotlight operator at a play, dimming the words that don't matter right now and shining light on the ones that do.
