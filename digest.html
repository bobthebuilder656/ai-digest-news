    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Multimodal AI

    A multimodal AI system can work with more than one type of input or output, understanding both text and images, for example, or generating text plus audio, instead of being limited to just one format. Show it a photo of your fridge and ask "what can I cook with this," and a multimodal model can actually look at the picture and reason about the ingredients, the way a person glances at the fridge and starts planning dinner, rather than needing everything typed out in words first.
