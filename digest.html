  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Transformer

    The transformer is the neural network design, introduced in 2017, that made modern LLMs possible. Its key trick is reading an entire piece of text at once and weighing how every word relates to every other word, instead of reading strictly left to right like a person following a line with their finger. Imagine reading a mystery novel and being able to instantly cross-reference every earlier clue at once, rather than only remembering the last page, that's roughly the advantage a transformer has over older, word-by-word AI designs.
