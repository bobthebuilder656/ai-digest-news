  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Large Language Model (LLM)

    A large language model is an AI system trained on huge amounts of text so it gets very good at predicting what word should come next in a sentence. Think of it like the autocomplete on your phone keyboard, but instead of guessing one word from a few you typed, it has read a giant slice of the internet and can guess entire paragraphs, essays, or answers. That one trick, done at massive scale, is what lets it write, summarize, translate, and hold a conversation. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are all built on top of an LLM.
