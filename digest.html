    Oracle adds a semantic layer to ground AI agents in the business context

    Oracle is expanding its Autonomous AI Lakehouse with a Business Semantic Layer designed to give AI agents a consistent understanding of enterprise data, a move aimed at improving the accuracy of AI-generated insights and helping organizations scale agentic applications across business functions. To ground AI agents in business context, the Business Semantic Layer uses catalog annotations, SQL annotations imported from Oracle AI Database, and AI-suggested analytic views to create reusable models of enterprise data that capture dimensions, measures, hierarchies, calculations, and key performance indicators (KPIs), Oracle said in a statement. These models can then be used across Oracle’s natural-language querying, data retrieval, and analytics capabilities, including Select AI, to help ensure that queries and insights are based on consistent business definitions.   13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Quantization

    Quantization shrinks an AI model by storing its internal numbers with less precision, trading a small amount of accuracy for a model that's significantly smaller and faster to run, sometimes even on a phone. It's similar to saving a photo as a smaller, more compressed file: some fine detail is lost, but for most everyday purposes it still looks essentially the same while taking up a fraction of the space.
