  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Diffusion Model

    A diffusion model generates images (or audio) by starting from random visual noise, like TV static, and gradually refining it, step by step, into a coherent picture that matches a text prompt. It's a bit like a sculptor starting with a rough, shapeless block and slowly chiseling away until a specific figure emerges, except the "chiseling" is dozens of small AI-guided cleanup passes. It's the technique behind most modern AI image generators, like Midjourney and DALL-E.
