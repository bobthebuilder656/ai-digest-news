    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    AI Agent

    An AI agent is a system that can autonomously take a sequence of actions, like browsing the web, running code, or calling other software, to accomplish a goal, rather than just replying to a single prompt with text. The difference is like asking a assistant one question and reading their answer, versus handing them a task, "book me a flight to Chicago next week," and letting them independently search sites, compare prices, and complete the booking on their own.
