  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Hallucination

    In AI, a hallucination is when a model confidently states something false, a fake statistic, a made-up citation, or an invented fact, because it's generating plausible-sounding text rather than actually retrieving something it verified. It's less like lying, which implies knowing the truth and hiding it, and more like a very confident student improvising a convincing-sounding answer on an exam question they never actually studied. It remains one of the central unsolved problems in trusting LLMs for anything high-stakes.
