  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Context Window

    The context window is the amount of text an AI model can "see" and consider at once, including your prompt, any documents you've shared, and the conversation history so far. It's like a person's short-term memory during a conversation: once you're past a certain point, earlier details start slipping away, and once a conversation exceeds the context window, the model genuinely stops being able to "remember" the earliest parts of it.
