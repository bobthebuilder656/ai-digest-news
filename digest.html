  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Prompt Engineering

    Prompt engineering is the practice of carefully wording your instructions to an AI model to get better, more reliable results. It's similar to learning how to phrase a request to a very literal-minded assistant: "summarize this in three bullet points for a 10-year-old" tends to get a much more useful answer than just "summarize this." It ranges from simple tricks like asking the model to "think step by step" to elaborate, carefully tested prompt templates used inside real production software.
