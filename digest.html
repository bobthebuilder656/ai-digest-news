  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Foundation Model

    A foundation model is a large, general-purpose AI model trained on broad data, meant to be adapted afterward for many different specific tasks rather than built for just one purpose. It's the AI equivalent of a general-purpose engine that can be dropped into a car, a boat, or a generator, rather than a part custom-built for only one machine. GPT, Claude, and Gemini are all foundation models that get fine-tuned or wrapped into many different products.
