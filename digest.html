    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Machine Learning

    Machine learning is the broader field of teaching computers to spot patterns in data and get better at a task through experience, instead of a programmer writing out every rule by hand. It's the difference between hard-coding "if the email contains the word 'lottery,' mark it spam" versus showing the computer thousands of real spam and non-spam emails and letting it work out the patterns itself. Modern AI, including every LLM, is a product built on machine learning techniques.
