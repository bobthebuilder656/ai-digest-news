    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Data Center

    A data center is a facility packed with servers and, for AI purposes, GPUs, that provides the raw computing power behind training and running AI models. If an AI model is the recipe, a data center is the industrial kitchen, rows of powerful "stoves" running around the clock, that actually cooks it at scale. The recent AI boom has triggered a global wave of new data center construction, along with the enormous power and water demand that comes with it.
