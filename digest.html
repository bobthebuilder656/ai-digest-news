    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Reinforcement Learning

    Reinforcement learning is a training method where an AI learns by trial and error: it takes an action, and gets rewarded or penalized depending on the outcome. It's essentially how you'd train a dog with treats, the dog doesn't understand English instructions, but it learns "sit" earns a treat and jumping on the guest doesn't, purely from repeated feedback. It's used both in robotics, like a robot learning to walk, and in shaping how chatbots respond to people.
