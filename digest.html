  12. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

    AGI describes a hypothetical AI that can understand or learn any intellectual task a human can, rather than being narrowly good at one thing. Today's AI is more like a brilliant specialist: a chess engine is unbeatable at chess but can't hold a conversation, and a chatbot can hold a conversation but can't drive a car. AGI would be more like a generalist who can pick up any of those skills. No system today is widely agreed to have reached it, but it's the north-star reference point in most debates about where AI is headed.
