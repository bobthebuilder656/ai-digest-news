    AI Safety

    AI safety is the field focused on preventing AI systems from causing unintended harm, spanning near-term, everyday issues like biased hiring tools or misinformation, up to longer-term concerns about highly capable autonomous systems acting in ways nobody wanted. It's the same instinct as safety engineering in any powerful technology, cars, airplanes, nuclear power, just applied to software that can now make its own decisions.
