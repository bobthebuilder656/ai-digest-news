  13. Today's Term

    One new AI word a day, explained simply — it adds up.

    Knowledge Distillation

    Knowledge distillation is training a smaller, faster "student" AI model to mimic the behavior of a larger, more capable "teacher" model. Think of a senior expert teaching an apprentice their best shortcuts and judgment calls, so the apprentice can work almost as well without needing the same decades of raw experience. It's how companies produce cheaper, quicker models that still perform reasonably close to their flagship, much larger versions.
